Who We Are

About Our Association

A proud community dedicated to neighbors helping neighbors since the early 2000s.

Our Mission

A Better Neighborhood,
For Everyone

The Frank Rushton Neighborhood Association exists to strengthen the bonds between neighbors, advocate for our community's interests, and create an environment where all residents feel safe, heard, and at home.


We work closely with Kansas City's city council, parks department, and local businesses to address concerns and champion improvements that benefit every household in our area.

What We Stand For

Our Core Values

Community First

Every decision we make puts the well-being of our residents at the forefront. We believe a strong neighborhood starts with strong relationships.

Open Communication

We keep our community informed through regular meetings, newsletters, and accessible leadership. Your voice matters and we're here to listen.

Sustainability

We champion green initiatives, tree planting programs, and responsible development that preserves what makes our neighborhood beautiful.

Safety & Security

Working with local law enforcement and city officials, we help maintain a safe environment for all — especially our children and elderly residents.

Vibrant Culture

From block parties to seasonal celebrations, we create opportunities for neighbors to connect, celebrate, and build lasting friendships.

Equity & Inclusion

Our association welcomes all residents regardless of background. We strive to make every voice heard and every neighbor feel they belong.
