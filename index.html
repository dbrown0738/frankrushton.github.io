About Us

The Frank Rushton Neighborhood Association exists to promote community engagement, neighborhood improvement, and collaboration among residents. We work to ensure our neighborhood remains safe, welcoming, and vibrant for everyone.

We are a volunteer-driven organization focused on strengthening civic engagement, improving neighborhood safety, and fostering meaningful connections between residents.
